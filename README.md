## Synopsis

[Keep Control](https://keep-control.eu/) was an industrial initial training network that was funded by the European Union's Horizon 2020 research and innovation programme under the **Marie Skłodowska-Curie** grant agreement No 721577.

In Kiel, Schleswig-Holstein, Germany, a [full-body mobility dataset](https://www.mdpi.com/2306-5729/7/10/136) was collected that comprised simulateneous recordings of a twelve-camera optical motion capture system and fifteen inertial measurement units (IMUs). The dataset provides a perfect opportunity to develop IMU-based algorithms for detecting of gait and other daily life-relevant movements.
